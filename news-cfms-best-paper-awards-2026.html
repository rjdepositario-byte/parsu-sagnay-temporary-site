Research · Best Paper Awards

Faculty researchers win three Best Paper awards at 2026 in-house review

Faculty researchers from the College of Fisheries and Marine Science (CFMS)–Sagñay won Best Paper awards at the 2026 Research Agency In-House Review.

ParSU Sagñay News Corner · September 23, 2026 · 2026 Research Agency In-House Review, Partido State University – Goa Campus, via Zoom · Photos: CFMS–Sagñay

Faculty researchers from the College of Fisheries and Marine Science (CFMS)–Sagñay Campus won Best Paper Awards at the 2026 Research Agency In-House Review. The event took place on September 23, 2026. Partido State University (ParSU)–Goa Campus hosted it via Zoom.

The awards honor their hard work and skill in research. They also show the team's drive to do studies that help people. Good research builds new knowledge. It also supports growth in our communities.

The whole CFMS family is proud of this win. We look forward to more wins like this. Each one proves our pledge to do excellent research and find new ideas.

Congratulations to our distinguished faculty researchers! You make CFMS proud!
