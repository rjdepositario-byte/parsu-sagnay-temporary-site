General questions

What is the Citizen's Charter?

It is the university's official guide to its frontline services under Republic Act No. 11032. For each service it lists who may avail, the requirements, the steps, fees, and processing time. These FAQs only cover the services the charter assigns to PSU campuses and that are available at Sagñay Campus.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · Citizen's Charter 2024 · PSU Campuses services · handbook pp. 242–341

Where can I see the full steps, requirements, fees, and processing time of a service?

Open the official Citizen's Charter 2024 (linked at the top of this page) and go to the handbook page shown under each answer. You may also ask the office in charge at the campus.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · Citizen's Charter 2024 · official PDF

Which offices serve students at Sagñay Campus?

Four campus offices handle the charter services listed here: the Dean's/Department Chair's Office of the College of Fisheries and Marine Science, the Campus Registrar, the Campus Library, and the Campus Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS), including the dormitory.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · Citizen's Charter 2024 · PSU Campuses services

Are there fees for campus services?

Many campus services list no fee in the charter. Some need paid items from outside offices, like a documentary stamp or an official receipt. Always check the “Fees to be Paid” column on your service's handbook page before you transact.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · Citizen's Charter 2024 · per-service pages

How long will my transaction take?

Each charter page gives a total processing time. Examples on this page: good moral character, about 9 minutes; transcript of records for other campuses, within 7 working days; board program admission, 7 working days with 20 minutes. Check your service's page for its exact time.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · Citizen's Charter 2024 · per-service pages

What if a service is not in these FAQs?

It may be a Goa (main) campus service, or a campus service with no verified Sagñay office yet — the campus medical and dental clinic, tilapia production and campus hostels, and rental of college equipment. We do not list services that are not available at Sagñay. For help, email sagnay.campus@parsu.edu.ph or call +63 938 866 0005. You may also rate our service through the Client Satisfaction link on the Contact page.

Admission and enrolment

How do I enroll as an incoming freshman?

Bring these to the Campus Registrar: Student Information Sheet (PSU-F-URO-39); High School Report Card (Form 138, original); Certificate of Good Moral Character (original); Certificate of Live Birth in PSA security paper (original); latest 2×2 picture with white background (3 copies); ParSUCAT result (PSU-F-GAP-04, original); and, for board programs, the Admission Result from the College. Face-to-face registration covers freshmen, regular and irregular students, transferees, returnees, shifters, and cross-enrollees in all campuses, including Sagñay.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSURO001 · Campus Registrar · handbook p. 320

How do I enroll as a regular student?

Get your SIAS credentials from the ICT Management Office (ICTMO). Log in to SIAS and pre-enroll your subjects. The University Registrar validates your enrolment, and the Dean's/Department Chair's Office monitors it until you are officially enrolled. Regular students follow the subjects and timeline of their program curriculum.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD002 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 244

How do I enroll as an irregular student?

Irregular students enroll through the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. The office will guide you on the subjects you may take for the semester and on your SIAS pre-enrolment.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD003 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 245

How do I enroll as a transferee from another campus or university?

You will be evaluated before enrolment. Prepare: TOR or Certificate of Grades from your last school (1 photocopy); Evaluation Form for Transferee (PSU-F-CollegeCode-01); Returnee/Shifter/Transferee's Slip (PSU-F-CollegeCode-02); Registration Permit (PSU-F-CollegeCode-03); and Suggested Subjects to Take (PSU-F-CollegeCode-12). The forms come from the College Dean's/Department Chair's Office. Get SIAS credentials from ICTMO so you can pre-enroll.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD004 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 248

How do I enroll as a returnee?

A returnee is one who comes back after temporarily leaving the university. Start at the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. The office will give you the forms and steps for returnee enrolment.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD005 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 251

How do I shift to another program, or shift from another program to CFMS?

A shifter transfers from one program to another. The charter has one service for shifters to other programs and another for shifters from other programs. Both are handled by the Dean's/Department Chair's Office, which will evaluate your case and give you the forms.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD006–007 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook pp. 254, 255

How do I get admitted to a board program like BS Fisheries?

Admission has three parts: the admission exam, an interview, and an evaluation. The Admission and Guidance Office gives the ParSUCAT results to the college. The Department Chair/Program Chair then sets your interview and evaluation schedule. Bring your ParSUCAT result (PSU-F-GAP-03). The college computes and submits the rank list. No fee is listed; total time is 7 working days with 20 minutes.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD001 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 243

Can I register or enroll online?

Yes. The charter provides online registration. You need SIAS credentials from ICTMO to log in and pre-enroll. Face-to-face registration at the Campus Registrar is also available.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSURO003 · Campus Registrar · handbook p. 330

Academic services

How do I add, change, or drop subjects?

Students may add or change subjects within the period set by the university. Go to the Dean's/Department Chair's Office and follow the steps and forms on the charter page for this service.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD008 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 258

How do I complete an incomplete (INC) grade?

Completion of INC grades is handled by the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. Visit the office to start the completion process for your subject.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD009 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 259

How do I get approval for a student activity on campus?

All student activities — academic, co-curricular, or extra-curricular — held on campus need approval. File your proposed activity with the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. The Campus OSAS also processes activity proposals and activity sheets.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD011 · Dean's Office · handbook p. 261; CMPSOSAS002 · Campus OSAS · handbook p. 306

How do I get approval for a student activity off campus?

Activities held off campus — local, regional, or national — also need approval from the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. File early and follow the requirements on the charter page.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD012 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 262

How do I apply for a scholarship or financial assistance?

Students who wish to apply may seek endorsement from the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. The office will tell you the requirements of the scholarship you are applying for.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD013 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 263

Who can help me with a special academic or personal case?

The Dean's/Department Chair's Office offers coaching and consultation for students with special cases — academic, co-curricular, and other student concerns. The office also accepts general inquiries and other requests from stakeholders.

How do I have my grades evaluated for internship or OJT?

Qualified students who will take OJT, practicum, or internship may request grade evaluation from the Dean's/Department Chair's Office.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD015 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 265

How do I apply for internship?

Internship is part of the curriculum. It trains and orients students in their field. Qualified students apply through the Dean's/Department Chair's Office.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD016 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 266

How do I get my clearance signed?

A student secures clearance at the end of the semester. Signing of clearance for students and alumni is done through the Dean's/Department Chair's Office.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD017 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 267

How do I apply for graduation?

Students who have completed all subjects in their curriculum may apply for graduation at the Dean's/Department Chair's Office.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD018 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 269

ICT materials and facilities for academic use may be reserved through the Dean's/Department Chair's Office.

Records and certificates

How do I request my transcript of records (TOR)?

File at the Campus Registrar's Office. Prepare: Request/Transaction Form (PSU-F-URO-37 & 37-A); latest 2×2 picture in business attire with white background (1 piece); documentary stamp (Php 30.00); long brown envelope; duly approved clearance (for undergraduates, PSU-F-URO-58); official receipt for the TOR fee; and your student or any valid ID. If a representative will claim it, add a signed authorization letter and valid ID. The charter lists processing within 7 working days for other campuses. The same service covers certification of general weighted average, transfer credentials, re-issuance of diploma, and TOR copies for other schools.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSURO002 · Campus Registrar · handbook p. 325

How do I get a certificate of good moral character?

This certificate is issued to students and alumni with no disciplinary case and no conviction for a crime involving moral turpitude during their stay in the university. Bring an official receipt from the Cashiering Office and one documentary stamp to the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. The office checks your record and releases the certificate in about 9 minutes total, with no service fee. Then proceed to the Registrar's Office for the dry seal.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD010 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 260

How do I request CAV or certifications of grades and units earned?

All ParSU students and graduates may request Certification, Authentication and Verification (CAV), Certification for Grades and Units Earned (CGUE), other certifications, and authentication of documents through the Office of the University/Campus Registrar.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSURO004 · Campus Registrar · handbook p. 336

How do I get an English as a medium of instruction certificate?

Request it from the Dean's/Department Chair's Office. Follow the steps and requirements on the charter page for this service.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSACAD019 · Dean's/Department Chair's Office · handbook p. 270

Library services

How do I apply for a library card?

New, transferee, and returnee students may apply at the Campus Library. Bring: Certificate of Enrollment (1 original copy, from the University Registrar's Office); validated school ID (from OSAS); and a recent 1×1 colored photo with white background. For a lost card, bring an affidavit of loss from a notary public. No fee is listed, and the steps take only a few minutes.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSLIB001 · Campus Library · handbook p. 277

How do I validate my library card?

Students may request validation of their library cards at the Campus Library. Bring your card and follow the steps on the charter page.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSLIB002 · Campus Library · handbook p. 278

How do I borrow books?

Present your validated library card and the books to be borrowed. Fill out the book card and the statistics form for borrowed books. The librarian checks the forms and releases the books. Faculty and staff may borrow with their employee ID; contract-of-service staff use a valid ID.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSLIB003 · Campus Library · handbook p. 279

How do I return books?

Return borrowed books at the Campus Library counter. The library receives books returned by students, faculty, and staff and records them on the returned-books form.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSLIB004 · Campus Library · handbook p. 280

Student services and dormitory

How do I apply for a student ID?

Students of PSU campuses submit a 2×2 picture with white background (white shirt or blouse) and the accomplished ID Information Sheet (PSU-F-OSA-07) to the Office of Campus OSAS. For a lost ID, bring an affidavit of loss (1 original copy) and the ID fee receipt from the Cashier's Office.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSOSAS001 · Campus OSAS · handbook p. 304

How do I get an activity proposal and activity sheet approved?

Students may request approval of their activity proposal and activity sheet from the Campus OSAS. The Dean's Office also approves proposed student activities on and off campus.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSOSAS002 · Campus OSAS · handbook p. 306

How do I apply for the campus dormitory?

Only bona fide students of PSU campuses with dormitories may apply, under the University Dormitory Policies and Guidelines (BOR Resolution No. 45, s. 2022). Get the Dormitory Applicant's Admission Profile (PSU-F-OSA-15) from the Campus Dormitory Manager. You also need 2 pieces of 2×2 pictures taken within the last 6 months, your schedule of classes, and your vaccination certificate or card. Ask first about room availability. If no room is free, you will be placed on the dormitory waitlist and informed when a room opens. Students who skip this process may be denied residence.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSOSAS003 · Campus OSAS/Dormitory · handbook p. 308

How do dormitory billing, renewal, and clearance work?

Every month, the dormitory manager handles billing for residents. Renewal of stay and dormitory clearance are part of the same service. Visit the Dormitory Manager or Campus OSAS for your billing, renewal, or clearance.

Citizen's Charter 2024 · CMPSOSAS004 · Campus OSAS/Dormitory · handbook p. 311
