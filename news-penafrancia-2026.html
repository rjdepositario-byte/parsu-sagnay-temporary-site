Faith and community

Students join Peñafrancia fluvial procession

Students of Partido State University–Sagñay Campus joined the faithful of St. Andrew Parish to mark the Feast Day of Our Lady of Peñafrancia on September 3, 2026.

ParSU Sagñay News Corner · September 3, 2026 · Photos by Eualalio Mangaoang

Viva la Virgen!

Students joined the faithful of St. Andrew Parish on September 3, 2026. They marked the Feast Day of Our Lady of Peñafrancia. Along the riverbank, the students prayed the Holy Rosary as the fluvial procession passed by. It was a quiet, meaningful witness of faith and devotion to Ina.

We thank St. Andrew Parish for the warm invite. We thank PPC Chairperson Ms. Nerissa A. Bocacao and Parish Priest Rev. Fr. Jesus B. Berdol. We also thank our OSAS Coordinator, Manuela R Encinas, and College Dean, Dr. Jonel M. Corral, for making this participation possible.

May Our Lady of Peñafrancia guide, protect, and bless the whole ParSU community.

Viva la Virgen de Peñafrancia!
