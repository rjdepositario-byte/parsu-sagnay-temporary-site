BS Fisheries

The flagship program of PSU-Sagñay focuses on instruction, research, extension, and linkages in the field of aquatic resources. It prepares students for professional careers in aquaculture, capture fisheries, fish processing, fisheries ecology, and entrepreneurship. The program emphasizes strong academic foundations, research engagement, practical fieldwork, and training in reputable institutions. It also incorporates enhancement classes to improve reading comprehension and licensure exam performance, career development activities, and sports for holistic student growth.

  • Aquaculture
  • Capture Fisheries
  • Fish Processing
  • Fisheries Ecology

Marine Science

BS Marine Biology

The Bachelor of Science in Marine Biology program is designed to develop graduates with a comprehensive understanding of marine life and its ecosystems. It equips students with the knowledge and skills necessary to address the dynamic nature of marine life and its ecosystem — applying scientific processes to problem-solving, contributing to the management of tropical marine resources, pursuing careers in marine biology, conservation, oceanography and fisheries, and promoting the sustainable utilization of marine resources. The curriculum supports the Philippines’ role as a global center for marine shore fish biodiversity and develops manpower for managing and enhancing these vital resources.

  • Marine Ecology
  • Coastal Systems
  • Oceanography
  • Conservation Science
